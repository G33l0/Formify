Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Android: Google Play Store → Subscriptions → Formify → Cancel
iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Formify → Cancel

I lost my documents after reinstalling. Can you recover them?

Unfortunately, all documents are stored locally on your device. When you uninstall the app, they are deleted. We recommend exporting important documents as PDFs and backing them up to cloud storage.

How do I remove the watermark?

Upgrade to Premium to remove watermarks from your PDFs. Go to Settings → Upgrade to Premium.

Can I use Formify for my business?

Yes! Formify is perfect for freelancers, small businesses, and entrepreneurs. All documents are professional and ready to send to clients.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund requests must be made through Google Play or App Store within their refund windows. We follow platform refund policies.
