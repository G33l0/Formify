1. Introduction

Welcome to Formify. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection

We prioritize your privacy:

  • All your documents are stored locally on your device
  • We do NOT upload your documents to any server
  • We do NOT have access to your invoices, receipts, or personal documents
  • Your business data, customer information, and documents remain 100% on your device

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Email address - Only if you subscribe to premium or contact support
  • Payment information - Processed by Google Play/App Store (we never see your credit card)

3.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information - Device type, OS version (for app compatibility)
  • App usage data - Features used, crashes (to improve the app)
  • Ad data - If you use the free version, AdMob collects data for targeted ads

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob (Ads)

Free version users see ads powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

4.2 Google Play / App Store (Payments)

Premium subscriptions are processed by:

  • Google Play (Android) - Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store (iOS) - Privacy Policy

5. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the app
  • To process your premium subscription
  • To send you receipts and payment confirmations
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To improve app features and fix bugs

6. Data Storage and Security

Your documents:

  • Stored ONLY on your device using SQLite database
  • We have ZERO access to your documents
  • If you uninstall the app, all data is deleted

Payment data:

  • We only receive confirmation that payment was successful
  • We never see your credit card or payment details

7. Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

You have the right to:

  • Access - Request what data we have about you
  • Delete - Request deletion of your data
  • Correct - Update incorrect information
  • Export - Get a copy of your data
  • Opt-out - Stop ads by upgrading to premium

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

10. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Contact us:

  • Email: privacy@formify.app
  • Contact form: Contact Page

11. Data Deletion

To delete all your data:

  1. Uninstall the Formify app (deletes all local documents)
  2. Cancel your subscription (if premium subscriber)
  3. Email us to delete your email from our records

Summary: We don't see your documents. They stay on your device. We only know your email if you subscribe. That's it. 🔒
